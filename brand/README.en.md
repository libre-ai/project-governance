platform. Public English surfaces project it without inventing claims.

**Understand. Verify. Contribute.**

## Meaning and voice

Owning the factory means being able to inspect published components, modify them
under their licences, choose where distributable software runs, retrieve data,
and verify documented decisions and limits. It does not mean infrastructure is
free, dependencies disappear, or control is absolute.

The voice is incisive, calm, and falsifiable. It challenges closed-factory
dependency rather than companies, communities, or geographies. A strong claim
is immediately followed by its mechanism, source, verification date, and limit.
Project maturity and state always come from `project.v1.yaml`.

Forbidden brand claims include “free”, “more complete than every competitor”,
“no dependencies”, “total security”, “fully explainable”, and “absolute
control”. This explicit prohibition does not make those phrases publishable.

## Family and visual semantics

`Libre AI` is the sole master brand. A product first appears as
`Libre AI <Product>`, using a name from the LEXICON. Products do not receive
independent logos, promises, or permanent status-like colours.

The visual concept is the **open factory**, expressed through open assembly:
visible pieces, joints, inputs, outputs, and controls. `@libre-ai/ui` owns the
visual implementation. It uses the Envol constructif palette and never forms a
gradient between jade and iris.

The candidate mark is the **bird** already used as the avatar of the `libre-ai`
GitHub organisation: a white bird in flight with a jade inner wing, preceded by
a trail of squares. It replaces the Workshop Gantry, which is no longer a
candidate (ADR-0033, amendment of 2026-10-13).
