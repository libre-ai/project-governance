# Portfolio consolidation and Forge product direction

Status: owner-approved direction on 2026-09-29; repository admission and publication pending their required checks and protected workflow.

## Context

The organization contains successive generations of repositories. Nineteen target repositories have documentation-only default branches and separate code-recovery branches. Historical governance and newer product framing need explicit reconciliation. Creating another repository topology before this admission would add competing authorities.

## Decision

1. Keep Libre-AI as a laboratory and open software ecosystem. Forge is its integrated product factory; applications and reusable capabilities retain independent value and ownership.
2. Consolidate existing repository families. Functional runtime names do not automatically create new repositories.
3. Prepare project-governance as the destination for portfolio doctrine and schemas-and-contracts for admitted shared contracts and SDK projections. Record individual transfers; do not declare the entire historical corpus superseded.
4. Use ai-work-supervision as the initial Forge product home. Map runtime, sandbox, policy, authorization and verification responsibilities without conflating them.
5. Qualify recovered code and its consumers before integration. Keep delivery, integration, distribution and verified user availability distinct.
6. Preserve sources and verify restoration before retiring references. Destructive operations and changes to protected publication controls require their exact scope and authorization.
7. Keep research private until approved material is deliberately promoted. This document contains a product decision summary, not a raw research or conversation export.

## Consequences and limits

Historical repository ADR numbers are not reused for the local F-series Forge design inventory. New runtime state and protocol details are design candidates requiring tests and review, not qualified implementations.

No existing license, provider, model, deployment profile or platform toolkit is silently replaced. No repository deletion, branch deletion, force push, unverified merge or protection bypass is authorized by this ADR. Ambiguous destinations in the register remain unresolved until responsibility and consumers are established.
