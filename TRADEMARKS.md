# Libre AI trademark policy

The open-source and content licences in this repository do not grant permission
to use Libre AI names, logos, product marks, certification marks or visual
identity except for reasonable nominative reference.

## Permitted nominative use

Without separate permission, you may make truthful statements such as:

- “based on Libre AI”;
- “compatible with Libre AI”;
- “fork of the Libre AI project”;
- references and links in documentation, reviews and dependency notices.

The reference must not imply sponsorship, certification, partnership or an
official distribution. It must be no more prominent than your own product or
organisation name.

## Permission required

Prior written permission is required to:

- name a product, service, company or domain with `Libre AI` or a confusingly
  similar expression;
- use a Libre AI logo as the logo or primary branding of a fork or service;
- describe an offering as “official”, “certified”, “validated”, “enterprise” or
  “supported by Libre AI”;
- alter a logo or use a mark in advertising, merchandising or certification;
- present compatibility in a way that suggests endorsement.

Forks must use distinct names and branding. Removal of the original marks does
not change the source-code licence obligations.

## Brand assets

Brand assets are not covered by EUPL, Apache or Creative Commons merely because
they appear next to licensed material. A figurative asset must not be published
until both independent controls are complete: its exact
`LicenseRef-Libre-AI-Brand-1.0` text is accepted by the owner, and a dated visual
similarity review is archived against EUIPO, INPI, WIPO and the named visual
reference set. The review is visual screening, not legal clearance.

Source code may contain an explicitly unpublished candidate so it can be
reviewed. A public package, website, profile, release or download must fail
closed while either control remains pending.

This policy does not claim that every referenced mark is registered. Clearance,
ownership and any INPI/EUIPO registration are managed separately from source
licensing.
